## 4. Anti-Theft & Anti-Piracy Protections

MultiHAT Academy protects intellectual property through active legal and technical safeguards:

1. **Dynamic Watermarking**: Every purchased e-book PDF is programmatically stamped with the buyer's email tiled diagonally across every page and a traceable footer license.
2. **Leak Attribution**: Embedded UTM-tracked resource URLs and unique token fingerprints identify the source of unauthorized distributions.
3. **Automated Monitoring**: Continuous web alerting monitors public indexers and file-sharing networks for unauthorized book distributions.
4. **Zero Tolerance for Scrapers & Re-sellers**: Any unauthorized scraping, packaging, or resale of course content will result in immediate DMCA takedown notices, payment gateway blacklisting, and legal enforcement.
